Money basics checklist

  • Emergency fund – easy-access cash for unexpected bills, built up before investing.
  • Pay off expensive debt – credit card and overdraft interest usually costs more than savings earn.
  • Get your employer's pension match – contributing enough to get the full employer contribution adds money you'd otherwise miss out on.
  • Use your ISAs – interest, dividends and gains inside an ISA are tax-free.
  • Give it time – investing is for five years or more; staying invested through ups and downs matters more than timing the market.
