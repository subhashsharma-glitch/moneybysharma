How the experiment works

  • Three separate books, each starting with $10,000 of fake money. Each puts a fixed dollar amount into every position it opens. No real money, broker or wallet is involved.
  • IA-ATR signals (since 12 Oct 2026): Opens a position when a chart-based model using Average True Range (a measure of how much a price typically moves) gives a BUY signal; closes on its SELL signal, stop or target.
  • X buzz (since 12 Oct 2026): top 3 bullish-trending tickers on X, $1,000 each; exit at 10 trading days, -8%, +20%, or bearish buzz turn. Fake money only.
  • Solana meme coins (since 26 Oct 2026): Fake-money paper strategy on Solana meme coins using public Jupiter prices; no wallet and no real orders. Each run the scanner reads Jupiter's trending, most-traded and top organic-score lists and keeps coins up at least 10% in 24h, not falling over the last hour, with at least $250k of 24h volume (stablecoins, stock-tracking coins and large-cap majors excluded). A rug check then requires Jupiter verification, an organic score of at least 70, at least $100k liquidity, a trading pair at least 24 hours old, mint and freeze authority renounced, top holders at or below 35%, any dev wallet at or below 10%, a 24h rise no larger than 300% and at least 1,000 holders. Coins that pass get a $500 paper position (maximum 10 open, at most 3 new per run). Fills use the Jupiter USD price plus a modelled 1% slippage and fee on entry and minus 1% on exit. A position is closed at +50% (take profit), -25% (stop), after 72 hours, or if it later fails the rug check (for example liquidity collapsing below $100k). Open positions are marked to the Jupiter price at the latest run; market value is shown before and after the modelled 1% exit cost, and unrealised P&L and equity use the after-cost value. Times are UK time.
  • Prices and marks. The ATR and X buzz books are marked to the last official daily close; the meme book uses the latest Jupiter price recorded by the experiment. Unrealised gains and losses are paper figures and can be out of date.
  • This is not a live record. It is a log updated from time to time, so it may be behind. Real trades could have filled at different prices.
