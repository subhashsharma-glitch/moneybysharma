Paper trading experiment

Two fake-money books, logged in public.

Experiment with fake money. Not advice, not a recommendation, not a signal. Past results don't predict future ones.

Data as of 23 October 2026, 13:33 UK time. Open positions marked to last close, 22 Oct 2026. Paper-trading experiment with fake money. Not financial advice.

How the experiment works

  • Two separate books, each starting with $10,000 of fake money and putting $1,000 into each position it opens. No real money is used.
  • ATR book: positions opened when a chart-based model (using Average True Range, a measure of how much a price typically moves) flags them.
  • X buzz book: top 3 bullish-trending tickers on X, $1,000 each; exit at 10 trading days, -8%, +20%, or bearish buzz turn. Fake money only.
  • This is not a live record. It is a log kept by hand and by script, updated from time to time, so it may be out of date. Prices are those recorded when each entry or exit was logged, including pre-market prices, and real trades could have filled at different prices.
  • Closed-trade P&L, unrealised P&L and equity at market are shown separately. Open positions are marked to the last daily close (official end-of-day closing prices, not live prices), so unrealised gains and losses can be days old and will change. Equity at market is cash plus open positions at that close. Start dates are the date of each book's first trade, not necessarily when it was set up.
