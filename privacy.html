Privacy

Short version: we don't use cookies, we don't track you and we don't collect your data.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. All fonts, scripts and data are served from this site itself – there are no third-party embeds.

Calculators

Calculator inputs stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

Email sign-up

The "weekly best 3 rates" sign-up is switched off ("coming soon") and collects nothing. If we launch it, we'll update this page first to explain what's collected and why.

Feedback

The feedback page opens a message in your own email app, addressed to hello@moneybysharma.co.uk. This site doesn't receive, store or process what you type, and nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app. Your message is used to improve the site, and your email address only to reply to you. Neither is shared or sold.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site – see the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in new tab).

Contacting us

You can email hello@moneybysharma.co.uk. If you do, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. They aren't added to any mailing list or shared.

External links

Links to other sites (for example GOV.UK or the FSCS) are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Information only – not financial advice. MoneyBySharma gives general information, not regulated financial advice, and is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Do your own research or speak to an FCA-authorised adviser before making decisions. With investments your capital is at risk and you may get back less than you put in. Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may change in future.
