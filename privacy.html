Privacy

Short version: this site sets no cookies and doesn't track you. The only things it sends anywhere are a feedback message or an email sign-up, and only if you press Send or Subscribe.

No cookies, no analytics

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. All fonts, scripts and data are served from this site itself – there are no third-party embeds. The exceptions are the optional feedback form and email sign-up, described below.

Calculators

Calculator inputs stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere.

Weekly money update (email sign-up)

If you sign up on the home page, the only personal data collected is your email address. The form sends it to Buttondown (buttondown.com), a third-party newsletter service that stores your address and sends the email for us. Buttondown first emails you a confirmation link, and you only join the list when you click it. We use your address only to send the weekly update, which is a short Monday email about what changed on this site that week. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you at hello@moneybysharma.co.uk. Your address is never sold or used for anything else. Buttondown has its own privacy policy (opens in new tab), and its pages may set cookies. Nothing is sent unless you tick the box and press Subscribe.

Feedback

If you use the feedback page, what you type is sent through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a third-party form service, which emails it to the site owner at hello@moneybysharma.co.uk. What is sent is your message, the type of feedback, the part of the site you chose, and your email address only if you give one. It is used to improve the site, and your email address only to reply to you. It is never sold. Your browser sends it to FormSubmit directly, and FormSubmit has its own privacy policy (opens in new tab), which says it may use cookies. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you'd rather not use the form, you can email the address above instead.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site – see the GitHub Privacy Statement (opens in new tab).

Contacting us

You can email hello@moneybysharma.co.uk. If you do, your email address and message are used only to reply to you. They aren't added to any mailing list or shared.

External links

Links to other sites (for example GOV.UK or the FSCS) are covered by those sites' own privacy policies.

Information only – not financial advice. MoneyBySharma gives general information, not regulated financial advice, and is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA). Do your own research or speak to an FCA-authorised adviser before making decisions. With investments your capital is at risk and you may get back less than you put in. Tax treatment depends on your individual circumstances and may change in future.
