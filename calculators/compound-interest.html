Compound interest calculator

See how simple and compound interest compare, what the AER is, and how long money takes to double.

Simple interest is paid only on the amount you first put in. Compound interest is paid on that amount and on the interest already added, so growth can build on itself. Savings accounts normally pay compound interest.
