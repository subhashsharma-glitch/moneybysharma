Annuity income calculator

Estimate yearly income from published market rates, for a given pot, age and annuity type.

This uses the same published reference rates as our annuities guide, from the Hargreaves Lansdown annuity rate table, quotes dated 5 Nov 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026. It is an estimate, not a personal quote. Your actual quote will differ.

This is the same calculator as on our Annuities guide, which also covers enhanced annuities, tax-free cash, drawdown and UK providers.

Annuity income estimate

Reference rates: Hargreaves Lansdown annuity rate table (opens in new tab), quotes dated 5 Nov 2026, checked 9 Nov 2026. These are the highest standard quotes HL's tool found across the providers it compares for a £100,000 purchase (average postcode, paid monthly in advance; joint life assumes a partner 3 years younger). We use the published figure for the nearest age at or below yours and apply it in proportion to the amount you'd use. We don't adjust for anything the source doesn't publish, so other combinations show "not available". Your actual quote will differ and is likely to be lower for small pots.

Information only, not financial advice. The figures are an illustration. This is not a quote, a forecast or a recommendation.
