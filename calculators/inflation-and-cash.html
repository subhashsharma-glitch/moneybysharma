Inflation and cash calculator

See how inflation and interest change what a sum of cash can buy over time.

If prices rise faster than the interest on your savings, your money buys less each year, even if the number in the account goes up. The latest UK CPI figure used on this site is 3.5% for 2026 Sep. The default rate in the calculator is an example, which you can change.
