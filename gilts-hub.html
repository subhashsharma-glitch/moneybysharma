Gilts

UK government bonds, gathered in one place: the main guide, four short articles and a calculator.

A gilt is a bond issued by the UK government. These pages explain how gilts work, how they are taxed, how they compare with savings, and how you can hold them. The calculator lets you compare a gilt held to maturity with a savings account after tax, using a price you enter.

This is information, not financial advice. It does not take your circumstances into account and is not a personal recommendation to buy, sell or hold any gilt or other product. Gilts can fall in price, and tax rules can change. If in doubt, speak to an FCA-authorised adviser.
